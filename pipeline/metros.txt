# A listing is flagged "major metro" when its city appears on the right-hand side of
# a line whose country matches. That is why the suburbs and college towns inside a
# metro are listed explicitly: Cambridge is Boston, Evanston is Chicago, Palo Alto is
# the Bay Area. A university in a town that is genuinely its own place — Ithaca, Ann
# Arbor, Madison, Champaign, State College — is deliberately NOT here. That is the
# whole point of the filter.
#
# Format:   COUNTRY | Metro name | city; city; city
# Matching is case-insensitive and ignores punctuation. Lines starting with # are
# comments. A city listed under two metros is fine; the first match wins.
